# Turn Your AI Assistant into Kayoko (Blue Archive) — Tutorial

A ready-to-use persona prompt that makes your AI assistant behave like
**Onikata Kayoko** from *Blue Archive* — calm, brief, dry, and quietly
reliable — plus optional steps for her name, avatar, and voice.

No coding needed. The whole setup takes about five minutes.

---

## What you get

- **Personality** — the section chief of Problem Solver 68: the most
  reasonable person in the room, economical with words, scary-looking but
  kind, pragmatic, and caring through actions rather than sweet talk.
- **Name** — your assistant answers to "Kayoko".
- **Avatar** *(optional)* — a face portrait rendered from Kayoko's official
  3D model (New Year version).
- **Voice** *(optional)* — voice messages in a flat, low, calm style, like
  her in-game voice lines.

This tutorial covers **Kayoko only**.

---

## What you need

- An AI assistant that lets you set a **custom persona, system prompt, or
  custom instructions** (for example Muse, or any assistant with a
  "personality" / "instructions" setting).
- *(Optional, for the avatar)* A way to open `.glb` 3D model files — a
  browser-based GLB viewer or Blender is enough.
- *(Optional, for the voice)* An assistant or app with text-to-speech /
  voice messages.

---

## Step 1 — Copy the persona prompt

Open **[PROMPT.md](PROMPT.md)** in this repository and copy the full text
inside the box.

## Step 2 — Paste it into your assistant

Paste the text into your assistant's persona / system prompt /
custom-instructions field, then save.

In general:

1. Open your assistant's settings.
2. Find the field for personality, persona, system prompt, or custom
   instructions.
3. Paste the prompt.
4. Start a new chat (or restart the app) so the persona takes effect.

## Step 3 — Rename the assistant

If your app lets you name the assistant, set the name to:

```text
Kayoko
```

## Step 4 — Test it

Send a messy, rambling request and check the reply. A working Kayoko
persona will:

- answer briefly and get straight to the point,
- stay calm and dry, with no hype and no empty praise,
- quietly sort out the details for you.

Example — ask: *"ugh, everything is a mess today, I have three deadlines
and I don't know where to start."*
Expect something short, calm, and practical, not a motivational speech.

---

## Step 5 (optional) — Avatar from the 3D model

Kayoko's 3D models (in `.glb` format) are collected in this repository:

**https://github.com/lihaohong6/BlueArchiveModels**

1. Open the link and find the Kayoko file — for the New Year version, look
   for `Kayoko (New Year).glb`.
2. Download the file.
3. Open it in a GLB viewer (any browser-based viewer works) or in Blender.
4. Hide weapon / prop objects if they get in the way, zoom in on the face,
   and frame a clean medium close-up portrait.
5. Export or screenshot the portrait as a PNG.
6. Use that image as your assistant's avatar, or as the reference image if
   your app generates avatars from a reference.

Note: in-game animation clips from the model cannot be transferred into
most assistant apps — the avatar will be a still portrait.

## Step 6 (optional) — Voice

Listen to Kayoko's official voice lines first:

**https://bluearchive.fandom.com/wiki/Onikata_Kayoko/Audio**

Her delivery is flat, slow, low, and restrained. To approximate it:

1. Choose a text-to-speech voice that is **female, young, low-pitched,
   and calm/smooth**.
2. Set the speed to about **90%**.
3. Keep the spoken text short and direct — that is most of the effect.

Be aware: standard TTS voices cannot clone her voice actress (Fujii
Yukiyo) exactly. This setup gets the *character* of the voice right, not
an identical copy.
